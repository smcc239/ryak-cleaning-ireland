## Compliance regime for data centres

Data centre environments operate under strict contamination and access controls. Our compliance approach aligns with ISO 14644 awareness for cleanroom cleaning, ensuring particulate generation is minimised during every task. Operatives follow anti-static protocols when working near racks, patch panels and raised-floor voids, using ESD-safe equipment and approved consumables throughout.

Controlled access procedures are central to our delivery. Every data hall entry is preceded by a method statement and risk assessment agreed with your facilities team. We maintain escorted access logs, adhere to your badge and biometric requirements, and brief teams on hot-aisle and cold-aisle restrictions before work begins. Documentation is structured for client audits, tenant reviews and third-party certification inspections.
