## Compliance regime for healthcare

Healthcare facilities operate under strict infection prevention and control requirements. Our cleaning protocols align with HIQA guidance for residential care settings and clinical environment standards for medical facilities. Every operative receives training in hand hygiene, personal protective equipment use, colour-coded equipment segregation and the correct application of disinfectants with validated contact times.

Documentation supports your clinical governance and regulatory inspection programme. We provide cleaning schedules, method statements, product safety data and completion records that demonstrate compliance during HIQA visits and internal audits. Cross-contamination prevention is embedded in every task, with equipment dedicated to specific zones and replaced or laundered according to agreed frequencies.
