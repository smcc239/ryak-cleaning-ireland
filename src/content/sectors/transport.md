## Compliance regime for transport

Transport depots and terminals are live operational environments with specific safety requirements. Our method statements address vehicle movements, charging infrastructure, maintenance pits and restricted zones. Operatives receive site-specific inductions and work under RAMS agreed with your depot or facilities manager before any cleaning activity begins.

COSHH-compliant product selection ensures safe use around fuel areas, electrical systems and passenger-facing surfaces. Documentation includes risk assessments, insurance certificates and completion records suitable for operator audit programmes and regulatory inspection. Scheduling protocols respect operational timetables, with overnight and early-morning slots structured to complete work before service commencement.
