## Compliance regime for education

Educational environments carry specific safeguarding and vetting obligations. Every Ryak operative assigned to a school or college holds current Garda vetting, maintained and renewed in accordance with Department of Education requirements. Teams receive child safeguarding briefings and understand the protocols for working in environments where minors are present.

Infection control has become a permanent priority in educational settings. Our cleaning protocols include enhanced disinfection of high-touch surfaces, colour-coded equipment to prevent cross-contamination between toilets and classroom areas, and adjustable frequencies during periods of elevated illness. Documentation supports your facilities audit programme and demonstrates compliance during board of management reviews.
