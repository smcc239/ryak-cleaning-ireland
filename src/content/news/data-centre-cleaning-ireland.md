Ireland's data centre sector continues to expand across Dublin, Cork and regional colocation sites. Cleanroom-aware maintenance is no longer optional — it protects uptime, supports audit requirements and reduces contamination risk in critical environments.

## Why data centre cleaning differs from office cleaning

Server rooms and white space require anti-static protocols, controlled access and documented method statements. Standard contract cleaning products and techniques can damage sensitive equipment or leave conductive residues. Teams must complete site inductions and work to agreed particle control standards.

## ISO 14644 and facility expectations

While not every data hall operates as a certified cleanroom, many Irish operators reference ISO 14644 principles for particulate control. Your cleaning contractor should understand raised floor voids, cable management zones and the difference between white space, grey space and plant areas.

## What to include in your specification

Define access windows, escort requirements, approved consumables and photographic evidence for audit trails.
