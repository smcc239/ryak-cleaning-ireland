Appointing a commercial cleaning contractor is one of the most visible procurement decisions a facility manager makes. The wrong choice shows up in washroom standards, audit findings and staff complaints long before contract renewal. This guide outlines what Irish organisations should verify before signing.

## Define your scope before you tender

Start with a written specification covering frequencies, zones, consumables and out-of-hours access. Daily [contract cleaning](/services/contract-cleaning/) for a multi-tenant office block differs sharply from periodic [industrial cleaning](/services/industrial-cleaning/) in a production plant. Match your tender to actual footfall, hygiene risk and presentation requirements rather than copying a generic template.

Include escalation procedures, quality audit frequency and reporting format. Procurement teams that define measurable standards upfront receive comparable quotations and fewer disputes during mobilisation.

## Verify accreditations and insurance

Accreditations are not decorative — they demonstrate that a contractor's management systems survive independent scrutiny. Look for ISO 9001 quality management, ISO 14001 environmental management, SafeContractor health and safety accreditation and, for construction handover work, Constructionline Gold registration.

Request current certificates, employer's liability and public liability schedules, and sample risk assessments and method statements. Reputable contractors supply these routinely for tender submissions. Ryak's full accreditation profile is published on our [accreditations page](/accreditations/).

## Insist on direct employment

Agency labour may be cheaper on paper, but it creates inconsistency in training, vetting and accountability. Directly employed operatives receive structured induction, uniform standards and supervision that casual labour pools rarely match. This matters in environments where cleaners work unsupervised — [offices and professional services](/sectors/offices-professional-services/) firms, healthcare settings and education campuses across Ireland.

Ask how turnover is managed, what vetting checks apply and whether the same team attends your site each week. A named account manager and stable crew reduce the onboarding churn that undermines standards on long-term contracts.

## Evaluate sector experience and references

Generic commercial cleaners struggle in specialist environments. If you manage a healthcare facility, confirm infection control protocols and colour-coded equipment segregation. For [office cleaning](/services/office-cleaning/) in client-facing professional firms, verify confidentiality awareness and secure access procedures.

Request references from comparable sites — similar size, sector and service frequency. Site visits to active contracts reveal more than a polished sales presentation.

## Mobilisation and ongoing governance

Agree a mobilisation timeline, induction requirements and a 90-day review checkpoint. Quality audits with photographic evidence and logged corrective actions give facilities managers documentation for internal reporting.
