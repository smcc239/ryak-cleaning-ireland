Practical completion and client handover depend on more than snagging lists and certificates. Dust-laden surfaces, trade residue on glazing and debris in communal areas undermine months of construction work in a single walk-through. A structured [builders clean](/services/builders-cleans/) programme protects your reputation and keeps handover dates on track.

## Builders clean versus sparkle clean

A **builders clean** removes construction debris, plaster dust and trade residue after primary works complete. It makes the site safe, workable and ready for second-fix trades, inspections and fit-out completion. Scope typically covers floor clearance, surface dusting, waste removal and initial window cleaning.

A **sparkle clean** is the final presentation clean before client handover. It addresses detail work — fixture polishing, floor finishing, internal glazing, sanitaryware and high-visibility surfaces — to showroom standard. Most commercial and residential projects require both stages, scheduled around your programme milestones rather than as a single visit.

Understanding this distinction prevents under-scoping tenders and last-minute mobilisation when the client walk-through is confirmed.

## Programme coordination with principal contractors

Post-construction cleaning must align with CDM site management, not compete with active trades. Agree cleaning phases during programme planning — which zones release when, access routes for waste removal and protection of completed installations.

Your cleaning contractor should attend site inductions, submit RAMS aligned with principal contractor requirements and coordinate with snagging teams so remedial cleans address specific items without repeating full sparkle scope. [Construction sector cleaning](/sectors/construction/) specialists understand this workflow; generalist contractors often mobilise too early or too late.

## External and specialist finishing

Handover presentation extends beyond internal areas. [Window cleaning](/services/window-cleaning/) for internal and external glazing, [power washing](/services/power-washing/) of paving and facades, and [deep cleaning](/services/deep-cleaning/) of plant rooms and common areas frequently form part of the handover pack. Coordinate these under one mobilisation where possible to avoid programme gaps before practical completion sign-off.

## Constructionline and contractor pre-qualification

Developers and principal contractors increasingly require pre-qualified suppliers before site appointment. Constructionline Gold registration streamlines this process by verifying insurance, health and safety credentials and management systems in a single database.

For main contractors managing multiple handovers, appointing a Constructionline-registered cleaning partner reduces onboarding friction across your project portfolio. Combined with ISO-certified processes and documented completion reports, this supports client acceptance at practical completion.

## Documentation for handover packs

Retain completion reports, photographic evidence and sign-off records for each cleaning phase. Commercial clients and building surveyors increasingly inspect cleanliness before accepting handover. Clear documentation demonstrates that presentation standards were met and provides a baseline if remedial works are required.
