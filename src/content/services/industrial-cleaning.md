## Industrial cleaning for demanding Irish environments

Manufacturing and industrial sites across Ireland need cleaning contractors who understand production schedules, safety protocols and audit requirements—not generic office cleaners with a mop and bucket. Ryak delivers industrial cleaning nationwide from our Dublin base, with the equipment, training and documentation industrial facilities managers expect when the HSE, auditors or customers walk the floor.

From food processing plants and pharmaceutical facilities to heavy manufacturing warehouses and logistics hubs, our industrial cleaning teams work around your operation rather than against it. Every engagement starts with a site survey, method statements and RAMS agreed before anyone sets foot on your production floor.

## What is included in our industrial cleaning service

Industrial cleaning scope varies significantly by sector, which is why we agree a detailed specification during onboarding rather than applying a one-size-fits-all checklist. Typical programmes include production line and plant area cleaning scheduled around shifts, warehouse and racking cleaning including high-level dust removal, loading bay and yard degreasing, plant room and utility area hygiene, confined space and high-level cleaning where access permits, and comprehensive shutdown deep cleans during planned maintenance windows.

Oil spill response and contamination cleaning can be integrated into scheduled programmes or mobilised urgently through our [emergency cleaning](/services/emergency-cleaning/) service. Food production clients often combine industrial cleaning with [commercial kitchen cleaning](/services/commercial-kitchen-cleaning/) and [ducting and ventilation cleaning](/services/ducting-ventilation-cleaning/) for a complete hygiene picture.

Industrial facilities managers choose Ryak because we employ trained operatives, produce method statements and RAMS for every site, and understand the difference between cleaning a reception and degreasing a production floor. ## Compliance, RAMS and our industrial cleaning process

Every industrial cleaning project follows a structured process beginning with site survey and risk assessment. We produce method statements and RAMS covering access equipment, chemical use, segregation from live production, PPE requirements and emergency procedures. Operatives are briefed on site-specific hazards before commencing work, and supervisors maintain contact with your facilities or production team throughout the shift.

For food production environments, we align with HACCP requirements and coordinate with your hygiene schedules. Manufacturing clients in regulated sectors benefit from documentation detailing areas cleaned, standards applied and recommendations for follow-up work.
