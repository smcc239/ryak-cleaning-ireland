## What is included in our contract cleaning service

Contract cleaning is more than wiping desks and emptying bins. Our scope is agreed during a structured site survey and set out in a written specification that covers frequency, methods, consumables and quality benchmarks. Typical contract cleaning programmes include daily or periodic cleaning of workstations, reception and client-facing areas, meeting rooms, kitchens and breakout spaces, washrooms with sanitisation and consumable replenishment, corridors, lifts and stairwells, and hard floor care or carpet vacuuming as scheduled.

We supply method statements and RAMS for every site, coordinate waste management and consumables where required, and schedule work out of hours when that suits your operation. For offices needing a specialist daily focus, see our dedicated [office cleaning](/services/office-cleaning/) service. Facilities preparing for audits or seasonal shutdowns often combine contract cleaning with periodic [deep cleaning](/services/deep-cleaning/) visits.

## Compliance, onboarding and how we work

Every new contract cleaning engagement follows a structured onboarding process. We conduct a site survey, agree scope and frequency, produce method statements and RAMS, and confirm access, keys, alarm codes and emergency contacts before the first visit. Operatives are briefed on site-specific requirements including secure areas, confidential waste handling and any healthcare or education protocols.

Quality is monitored through scheduled audits against your agreed specification. Findings are reported to your account manager with corrective actions tracked to closure. For healthcare and education environments, we align with your infection control and hygiene policies. Manufacturing and industrial clients benefit from our experience scheduling around production windows through our [industrial cleaning](/services/industrial-cleaning/) capability.
