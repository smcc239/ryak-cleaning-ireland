## 24/7 emergency cleaning response across Ireland

When fire, flood or contamination strikes, you need a contractor who can mobilise immediately—not one who returns your call on the next working day. Ryak's emergency cleaning teams operate 24/7 across Ireland from our Dublin base, responding to fire damage, flood recovery, oil spills and urgent pre-inspection cleans with the equipment, documentation and specialist capability commercial and industrial incidents demand.

Emergency cleaning is not standard contract work accelerated—it requires different skills, different equipment and the ability to scope, document and execute under pressure while insurers, loss adjusters and regulatory bodies await evidence of competent response. Ryak has supported Irish businesses through incidents for over 20 years.

## What is included in our emergency cleaning service

Emergency cleaning scope is assessed on arrival and agreed with site management before work commences. Our response covers fire and smoke damage cleaning including soot removal and odour treatment, flood and water damage recovery with extraction and drying coordination, oil spill containment and cleaning for industrial and commercial sites, urgent pre-inspection deep cleans when environmental health or audit deadlines cannot move, post-incident commercial kitchen cleaning and extract system recovery, and nationwide mobile deployment with insurance documentation support throughout.

Food production and catering incidents often require coordination between emergency cleaning, our [commercial kitchen cleaning](/services/commercial-kitchen-cleaning/) specialists and [ducting and ventilation cleaning](/services/ducting-ventilation-cleaning/) teams to restore operations safely. Industrial contamination may integrate with our [industrial cleaning](/services/industrial-cleaning/) capability for production floor recovery.

## Process, documentation and insurance coordination

Every emergency cleaning engagement follows a structured response. We confirm site safety and access, assess scope on arrival, agree priorities with site management and insurers where involved, produce method statements for ongoing works, and maintain photographic documentation throughout restoration. Progress reports support insurance claims and regulatory communication without you chasing updates from multiple contractors.

For healthcare and food production clients, we align with infection control and HACCP requirements during recovery. Industrial incidents include appropriate chemical handling documentation and environmental reporting support where contamination affects drainage or surrounding areas.
