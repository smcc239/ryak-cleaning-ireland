## Professional commercial kitchen cleaning for Irish food businesses

Commercial kitchen cleaning is not a cosmetic exercise. In Ireland's food service and production sector, grease accumulation in extract systems is one of the leading causes of kitchen fires, failed environmental health inspections and insurance claim complications. Ryak Cleaning Ireland provides specialist kitchen deep cleaning that addresses the full hygiene and compliance picture, not just visible surfaces.

Our Dublin-based operation has supported restaurants, hotels, hospital catering departments, school kitchens, food manufacturing plants and contract caterers across the island of Ireland for over 20 years. Every kitchen clean is planned around your service hours, documented to audit standard and delivered by directly employed, trained operatives.

## Why TR19 grease extract cleaning matters

The TR19 standard sets the benchmark for grease extract hygiene in commercial kitchens. Insurance providers, fire risk assessors and environmental health officers increasingly expect evidence that extract systems have been cleaned to this standard, with documented proof of the work carried out.

Ryak's kitchen cleaning teams strip, degrease and clean canopies, filters, duct runs and fan units using methods and access equipment appropriate to your installation. We provide photographic evidence before and after cleaning, along with certification reports that you can retain for audits, renewals and inspections.

If your kitchen shares ductwork with other tenants or sits within a managed building, we coordinate access and method statements in advance so there are no surprises on the night of the clean.

## Deep kitchen cleans beyond the extract system

A comprehensive commercial kitchen deep clean covers every surface that accumulates grease, biofilm and contamination over time. Our standard deep clean scope includes:

- Canopy interiors, exteriors and filter banks
- All cooking equipment including ovens, ranges, fryers and combi units
- Preparation benches, sinks and splashbacks
- Floor degreasing including drains and gullies
- Wall and ceiling cleaning to remove grease film and carbon deposits
- Cold storage surrounds and loading areas where applicable

For food production environments, we align our cleaning protocols with HACCP requirements and can integrate with your existing hygiene schedules and audit cycles.

## Certification and compliance documentation

Every commercial kitchen cleaning project includes documentation designed for the people who need to see it: facilities managers, environmental health officers, fire assessors and insurers. Our reports detail the areas cleaned, the standards applied, photographic evidence and any recommendations for follow-up work or increased cleaning frequency.

This documentation has helped clients across Ireland pass reinspections, satisfy insurance conditions and maintain fire safety compliance without costly downtime.
