## What is included in our office cleaning service

Office cleaning scope is agreed during a site survey and documented in a specification your facilities team can audit. Standard programmes include desk and workstation cleaning with appropriate sanitisation products, meeting room and boardroom presentation cleaning, reception and client-facing area maintenance, kitchen and breakout space hygiene including appliances and surfaces, washroom sanitisation with consumable supply and replenishment, and carpet vacuuming or hard floor mopping on agreed frequencies.

We also offer integrated floor care programmes through our [floor cleaning services](/services/floor-cleaning/) team, covering scrubbing, polishing and restoration on a scheduled basis. For broader facilities scope including corridors, car parks and communal areas, many clients choose our full [contract cleaning](/services/contract-cleaning/) package with a dedicated account manager.

## Compliance, confidentiality and our cleaning process

Office cleaning onboarding starts with a site survey covering scope, frequency, access arrangements and any confidentiality or data protection requirements. We produce method statements and RAMS, confirm keyholding and alarm procedures, and brief operatives on site-specific rules before the first visit. Quality is monitored through scheduled audits with findings reported and corrective actions tracked to closure.

For firms in regulated sectors, we align with your internal policies and can coordinate cleaning around sensitive periods such as year-end, client visits or board meetings. Education clients with office and admin space benefit from our experience across [education sector cleaning](/sectors/education/) environments where safeguarding and access control apply.
