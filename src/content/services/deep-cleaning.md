## Deep cleaning when standard schedules are not enough

Daily contract cleaning keeps premises ticking over, but it cannot reach everything. Grease accumulates on high-level surfaces, biofilm builds in void spaces, and presentation standards slip gradually until a client visit or audit exposes the gap. Ryak provides deep cleaning across Ireland for facilities preparing for audits, handovers, seasonal shutdowns or simply restoring standards that daily routines no longer achieve.

From Dublin offices and healthcare facilities to manufacturing plants and schools nationwide, our deep cleaning teams tackle the intensive work your regular schedule was never designed to cover. Every project is scoped during a site survey, scheduled around your operation, and documented with photographic evidence on completion.

## What is included in our deep cleaning service

Deep cleaning addresses areas and contamination that scheduled maintenance misses. Our scope is tailored to your facility but typically includes full facility deep cleans covering every agreed zone, high-level and hard-to-reach area cleaning including ceilings, pipework and light fittings, confined space cleaning where access permits, change-of-use and tenant handover cleaning, post-construction dust and residue removal, and intensive washroom, kitchen or production area sanitisation.

We integrate with your existing [contract cleaning](/services/contract-cleaning/) programme where clients want periodic deep cleans on a quarterly or bi-annual basis rather than one-off projects. Manufacturing and industrial clients often combine deep cleaning with our [industrial cleaning](/services/industrial-cleaning/) capability for production floor degreasing and plant room hygiene.

## Compliance, process and how we deliver deep cleaning

Every deep cleaning project follows a structured process. We conduct a site survey, agree scope and priorities, produce method statements and RAMS where required, and schedule work during evenings, weekends or shutdown windows to minimise disruption. Operatives are briefed on site-specific requirements including healthcare infection control, education safeguarding access rules or manufacturing segregation from live production.

On completion, you receive photographic reporting and sign-off documentation detailing areas cleaned, methods applied and recommendations for follow-up work. Healthcare clients benefit from our experience in [healthcare sector cleaning](/sectors/healthcare/) environments. Construction handover projects often coordinate with our [builders cleans](/services/builders-cleans/) team for a seamless transition to client occupation.
