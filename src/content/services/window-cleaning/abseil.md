## Safety, RAMS and our abseil cleaning process

Every abseil window cleaning project follows a structured safety process. We survey anchor points and facade configuration, produce method statements and RAMS covering exclusion zones, weather protocols, rescue procedures and occupant communication, and coordinate with building management and security before the first operative descends. Ground-level exclusion zones are managed throughout the drop with clear pedestrian routing.

Operatives inspect equipment before every session and work to IRATA standards for rope access operations. Documentation is retained for your records and available for insurer or auditor review.
