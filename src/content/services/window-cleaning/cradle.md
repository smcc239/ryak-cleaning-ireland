## Safety, RAMS and our cradle cleaning process

Every cradle window cleaning session follows a structured safety process. We conduct pre-use BMU inspections, confirm weather conditions within agreed limits, produce method statements and RAMS covering exclusion zones and emergency procedures, and coordinate with building management and security before the cradle leaves the roof level. Ground-level exclusion zones are managed throughout the traverse with clear pedestrian routing.

Documentation is retained for your compliance files and available for insurer or auditor review.
