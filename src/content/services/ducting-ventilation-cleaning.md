## Compliance, TR19 certification and our process

Every duct cleaning project follows a structured process aligned with TR19 and industry best practice. We survey the installation, agree access methods, produce method statements and RAMS, and schedule work during agreed windows—typically overnight for active kitchens. Canopy interiors, filter banks, duct runs and fan units are stripped, degreased and cleaned using methods appropriate to your system configuration.

On completion you receive TR19-compliant reports with before and after photographs, measurements where required, and recommendations for follow-up frequency based on cooking volume and grease accumulation rates.
